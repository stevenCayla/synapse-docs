The clinical research terms used in Synapse. In the guides, they are underlined with dots: hover over them to display the definition.

## 2

- **21 CFR Part 11** — US regulation (FDA) on electronic records and electronic signatures.

## A

- **ANSM** — French National Agency for the Safety of Medicines and Health Products (Agence nationale de sécurité du médicament et des produits de santé); registers studies (ID-RCB) and authorizes category 1 RIPH.
- **ARC** — Clinical research associate (CRA, monitor; Attaché de recherche clinique in French): checks data quality (monitoring) or helps with data entry on site.
- **Audit trail** — Tamper-proof log of every creation, modification or deletion: who, when, old and new value, reason.

## B

- **BPC** — Good clinical practice (Bonnes pratiques cliniques in French, GCP in English): the ICH E6 standard for conducting clinical trials.

## C

- **CDISC** — Consortium that defines clinical data exchange standards (ODM, SDTM).
- **CPP** — Ethics committee (Comité de protection des personnes in French, similar to an IRB or REC): issues a mandatory opinion before any research involving human participants.
- **CRA** — Clinical research associate (ARC in French): checks data quality (monitoring) or helps with data entry on site.
- **CRF** — Case report form. eCRF: its electronic version, made up of forms.
- **CTR** — Clinical Trials Regulation, European Regulation 536/2014 on clinical trials of medicinal products.

## D

- **D0** — Day 0 (J0 in French): day of enrollment (or randomization); visits are scheduled relative to D0.

## E

- **EDC** — Electronic data capture: software for the electronic collection of clinical study data.
- **eIDAS** — European regulation on electronic identification and qualified electronic signatures.

## G

- **GCP** — Good clinical practice (BPC in French): the ICH E6 standard for conducting clinical trials.
- **GDPR** — General Data Protection Regulation (RGPD in French).

## H

- **HDS** — Certified health data host (Hébergeur de données de santé in French): French certification required to host health data.

## I

- **ID-RCB** — Registration number of a study with the ANSM, in the format 2026-A00000-00.

## J

- **J0** — Day 0 (D0 in English): day of enrollment (or randomization); visits are scheduled relative to J0.

## M

- **MDR** — Medical Device Regulation, European Regulation 2017/745 on medical devices.

## O

- **ODM** — Operational Data Model: CDISC format for exchanging and archiving study data and metadata.

## P

- **PI** — Principal investigator: the physician responsible for the study at a site.

## Q

- **Query** — Question sent to the site about a doubtful or missing data point: opened, answered, then closed.

## R

- **RBQM** — Risk-based quality management: monitoring targeted according to the risk of sites and data.
- **Requête** — French term for a query: question sent to the site about a doubtful or missing data point, opened, answered, then closed.
- **RGPD** — French acronym for GDPR, the General Data Protection Regulation.
- **RIPH** — Research involving the human person (Recherche impliquant la personne humaine, Jardé law): category 1 (interventional), 2 (minimal risk) or 3 (observational).
- **RNIPH** — Research not involving the human person (Recherche n'impliquant pas la personne humaine), for example on data that has already been collected.

## S

- **SDTM** — Study Data Tabulation Model: CDISC format for tabulated data for regulatory submission.
- **SDV** — Source data verification: comparison of the entered data with the patient's source record.
