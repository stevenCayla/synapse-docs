## 1. Open a query

On a field, click the bubble icon → **New query**. Restricted to monitors (CRAs) and Data Managers.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Type** \* | `Missing data` | Missing, inconsistent, out of range, other. |
| **Question** \* | `Weight at D0 not entered` | Addressed to the site; visible to the site's investigators. |

## 2. Answer a query

The investigator receives the query in **My tasks** and on the field concerned.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Answer** \* | `Value corrected according to the source record` | Correct the data (with a reason) or explain why it is correct. |

:::tip[Automatic queries]
Warnings accepted during an import create one query per row, handled the same way.
:::

## 3. Close the query

The author reviews the answer and closes the query, or reissues it.

**What you should see**

* A closed query remains viewable in the field's history.
* The site's open query counter goes down.

## 4. Verify source data (SDV)

According to the study's monitoring strategy: 100%, risk-based (RBQM) or centralized.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Fields to verify** | — | With RBQM, only critical fields and at-risk sites are offered. |
| **Mark as verified** | SDV checkbox | Per field or per form. A later change cancels the verification. |
