This guide is for site **investigators** and **clinical research coordinators**. You only see the patients of your sites.

## 1. Enroll a patient

**Patients** → **Enroll a patient**.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Site** \* | `Lariboisière Hospital` | Suggested automatically if you are attached to only one site. |
| **Patient number** \* | `LARIB-01-003` | Generated by Synapse: site code + sequence number. It is never reused. |
| **Consent date** \* | `14/11/2026` | Must precede any data entry. |
| **Eligibility criteria** \* | check each criterion | The list reflects the study's inclusion and exclusion criteria. An unchecked criterion prevents enrollment. |

**What you should see**

- The patient appears in the list with the "Enrolled" status.
- Their visit schedule is calculated from D0.

## 2. Open a visit

Patient record → **Visits** tab. Each visit shows its target date and its tolerance window.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Visit date** \* | `14/12/2026` | A date outside the window is accepted but flagged "out of window" and tracked. |
| **Unscheduled visit** | `+ Unscheduled visit` | For an unplanned consultation: choose the forms to complete. |
| **Log forms** | `Adverse events` | AEs and concomitant medications: available at any time, outside the schedule. |

## 3. Fill in a form

Click a form in the visit.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Save** | `Ctrl + S` | Saves the draft. Empty required fields are flagged without blocking. |
| **Inline checks** | — | An out-of-range or inconsistent value is shown in orange with the rule's message. |
| **Calculated fields** | `BMI, age` | Calculated automatically, not editable. |
| **Edit a value** | reason required | After the first save, any change requires a reason, kept in the audit trail. |

:::note[Form statuses]
Empty → In progress → Complete → Verified (SDV) → Signed → Frozen. The colored dot shows the status in the schedule.
:::

## 4. Sign

The principal investigator signs the complete forms, by visit or by patient.

| Field | Example | What it is for |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Meaning** \* | `I certify the accuracy of the data` | The meaning of the signature, recorded with it. |
| **Password** \* | your password | Re-authentication required for each electronic signature. |

**What you should see**: signed forms show the signer's name and the timestamp.
