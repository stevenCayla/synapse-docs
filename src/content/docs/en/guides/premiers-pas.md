## Your account

Your administrator sends you an invitation. The first time you sign in, Synapse asks you, in order, to:

1. **Set up two-factor authentication.** Scan the QR code with an app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
2. **Keep your backup codes.** They let you sign in if you lose your phone. Store them somewhere other than your computer.
3. **Choose your password.** Remember it: it is also used for **electronic signatures** (data import, form validation).

## Essential or Advanced mode

The study creation wizard offers two modes, which you can switch at any time from the header without losing what you have entered.

| Mode | Steps | When to use it |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Essential** | 6 | Create a study quickly. Regulatory, eCRF and visit schedule are picked up in the checklist after creation. |
| **Advanced** | 12 | Configure everything upfront, notably uploading the consent form so that identifying data can be kept at import. |

## How Synapse saves

- **Autosave**: each field is saved a few seconds after you type. The window footer shows "Draft saved · just now".
- **Continue** validates the step. If a value is rejected, the message appears below the field concerned.
- **Closing the window loses nothing**: the **New study** button then offers to resume the draft.

## Reading the Completeness panel

At the bottom of the left column, three counters summarise what remains to be done.

| Counter | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Blocking** | Prevent the study from being launched. |
| **Before production** | To be resolved before go-live; they do not block creation. |
| **Warnings** | For information only. |

:::tip[Click a line]
Each completeness item takes you straight to the field concerned.
:::
